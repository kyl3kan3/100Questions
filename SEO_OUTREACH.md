Updated: 2026-07-29

## Objective

Earn relevant editorial links to useful research and product pages from
publishers that already rank for AI visibility, AEO, GEO, and LLM SEO queries.
The outreach asks for an independent evaluation or a factual correction, never
for a paid or undisclosed link.

the campaign goal. The priority is relevant, editorially controlled referring

## Personalization checklist

Before sending any message:

1. Confirm the article is still live and the stated category is current.
2. Address the named author or editor only when the publisher verifies that
   identity; otherwise use the editorial or product-listing route.
3. Mention one specific section, criterion, or observation from the article.
4. Choose one primary asset that helps that article's reader.
5. Offer review access or factual evidence, not a reciprocal or paid link.
6. Do not claim representative sampling, guaranteed rankings, consumer-chat
   parity, or causation that the published methodology does not support.
7. Send at most one concise follow-up, seven to ten business days later.

## Tracking rules

- Keep every row in `SEO_OUTREACH_PROSPECTS.csv` at `Draft only` until a human
  approves sending.
- Record the actual contact route before outreach. Do not scrape or store
  private personal contact information.
- After sending, record the date, recipient route, response, destination URL,
  anchor text, and whether the placement is editorial or sponsored.
- Decline paid links, undisclosed placements, link exchanges, and requests for
  fabricated reviews.
- Recheck Ahrefs monthly for new and lost referring domains, but judge success
  by relevance and qualified referral traffic rather than DR alone.
