Updated: August 28, 2026

This map differentiates pages that can legitimately coexist. It is an editorial and internal-linking contract, not evidence of cannibalization. No valuable URL should be consolidated or redirected until Search Console shows the same queries repeatedly splitting impressions, clicks, and position between pages with no distinct conversion role.

## Anchor and consolidation rules

- Link to `/ai-search-visibility-tool` with product-intent anchors such as “AI search visibility tool,” “frozen 100-answer benchmark,” or “measure mentions and citations.”
- Link to `/aeo-vs-geo` when the reader needs a terminology or program-scope decision, not as a generic “learn more” link.
- Link to optimization guides with the specific job: answer selection for AEO, entity/source presence for GEO, retrieval/citation for LLM SEO, and metrics for AI visibility.
- Keep tool roundups distinct: category routing belongs on `/ai-visibility-tools`; representative vendor comparison belongs on `/ai-seo-tools`; AEO-specific buying criteria belongs on `/answer-engine-optimization-tools`.
- Do not create additional thin pages for AEO, GEO, LLMO, or AI SEO synonyms.
- Keep `/pricing` focused on package choice; product scope belongs on `/ai-visibility-audit` and methodology belongs on `/ai-search-visibility-tool`.
- Keep the OAI-SearchBot guide narrow. Broader readiness belongs on `/ai-visibility-checker`, while optional llms.txt generation belongs on `/llms-txt-checker`.
