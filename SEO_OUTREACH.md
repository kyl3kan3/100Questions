domain-rating-only homepage links. Ahrefs currently detects 14 referring

Thanks,
Kyle

## Quality rules

- Personalize every message to the target page and its evaluation criteria.
- Ask for evaluation or correction, never a paid undisclosed placement.
- Link to the most relevant internal asset rather than defaulting to the
  homepage.
- Do not claim consumer-ChatGPT parity, guaranteed rankings, or representative
  sampling.
- Record the send date, response, published URL, destination URL, anchor text,
  and whether the placement is sponsored or editorial.
- Stop after one follow-up if there is no response.
